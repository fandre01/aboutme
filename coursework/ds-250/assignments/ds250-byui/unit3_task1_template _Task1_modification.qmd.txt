_First I went over to the data viewer, I found these mistakes. _

airport_name: Blank  
num_of_delays_carrier: 1500+,(wrong data type)
num_of_delays_late_aircraft: -999, (wrong data type)
minssutes_delayed_nas: -999, need to be fix

_I replace blank with np.nan. I replace -999 with np.nan
I replace 1500+ to 1500 because can be at leat 1500.



```{python}
df = df_dirty
df.airport_name = df.airport_name.replace("", np.nan)
df.num_of_delays_carrier = df.num_of_delays_carrier.replace("1500+", 1500).astype(int)
df.num_of_delays_late_aircraft = df.num_of_delays_late_aircraft.replace(-999, np.nan)
df.minutes_delayed_nas = df.minutes_delayed_nas.replace(-999, np.nan)
df
```
