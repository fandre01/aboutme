_The chart shows that the name Andre had varying popularity through the decades.

```{python}
# Q1
my_name = df.query('name == "Andre"')
#Count how often your name appears per year
trend = my_name.groupby('year', as_index=False)['Total'].sum()
(
    ggplot(trend, aes(x= 'year', y = 'Total'))
    + geom_line(color='blue')
    
    + scale_x_continuous(limits=[1950, 2020], format='d')
    + geom_vline(xintercepts= 2000, color= 'red', linetype='')
    
)
```

