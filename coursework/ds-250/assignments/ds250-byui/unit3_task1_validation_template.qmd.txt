__Pick any two baseball teams and compare them using a metric of your choice (average salary, home runs, number of wins, etc). Be creative! _Based on the graph, both teams’ average salaries increased over time, but the Yankees consistently had higher average salaries than the Red Sox. This may indicate that New York historically invested more in high-salary players._


```{python}
teams_of_interest = ['NYA','BOS']
team_salaries = salaries.query("teamID in @teams_of_interest")


avg_salary= (
  team_salaries
    .groupby(['teamID','yearID'])['salary']
    .mean()
    .round(2)
    .reset_index()
    .sort_values('salary',ascending=False))

avg_salary.head()
```
