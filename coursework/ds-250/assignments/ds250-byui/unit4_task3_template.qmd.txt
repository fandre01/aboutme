## QUESTION 1

__Create 2-3 charts that evaluate the relationships between each of the top 2 or 3 most important variables (as found in Unit 4 Task 2) and the year the home was built.__ Describe what you learn from the charts about how that variable is related to year built.   

_1. Live Area vs. Year Built
The first chart shows that newer homes tend to have larger living areas. Homes built before 1940 generally have much smaller square footage, while homes built after 1960 show a strong upward trend. This suggests that over time, homebuyers and builders began favoring larger homes, possibly because of growing families and higher living standards.

_2. One-Story Architecture vs. Year Built
The second chart indicates that the likelihood of a home being one-story changes over the years. Older homes show more variation, but around the mid-1900s the trend becomes more stable. This likely reflects changes in architectural preferences—certain periods favored one-story ranch-style homes, while others saw more two-story builds.

_3. Number of Bathrooms vs. Year Built
The third chart shows a clear increase in the number of bathrooms in newer homes. Early homes often had only one bathroom, but the number grows steadily with each decade. This matches historical changes in home design as indoor plumbing improved and modern buyers expected multiple bathrooms as a standard feature._

```{python}
#live area  vs year built
p1= (
    ggplot(df, aes(x='yrbuilt', y='livearea')) +
    geom_smooth(method="loess",color="red",span=0.3)
    + scale_x_continuous(limits=[1880,2000], format="d")
  
    
)
```
